## Several stations on one hole do not run from central control
1. In the central control software, run a decoder line diagnostic on the affected path.
2. Check whether the affected decoders share a common two-wire path segment.
3. Inspect splices on that segment for water ingress or damage, especially after a storm.
4. Check surge protection and grounding on that path.
Safety: if there are signs of lightning damage, burning smell, or exposed wiring, do not work on the equipment. Contact a qualified technician.

## Single station does not run
1. Confirm the decoder address in the central control software.
2. Test the solenoid connection at the decoder.
