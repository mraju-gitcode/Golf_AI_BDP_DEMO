# GolfPM demo story (synthetic)

All people, courses, companies, orders and part numbers are invented. Domains use example.com.
This is the single source for the test cases, the synthetic generator and the demo script.

## Setting

It is late November 2026. Golf courses are planning winter maintenance and spring renovations,
and the GolfPM shared mailbox at Rain Bird receives emails from courses and distributors.
Order status is the most common request.
Data is as of the morning order feed: **2026-11-27 06:00 ET**.
