## Section 2.1 Golf rotors
Rotors are covered against defects in materials and workmanship for 3 years from the installation date when installed and maintained per published instructions.

## Section 2.2 Decoders and field controllers
Covered for 5 years from installation. Lightning and surge damage are excluded.

## Section 3 Required information
Course contact (name and phone), affected part numbers and quantities, installation date, and a description of the failure. Photos are helpful.

## Section 4 Exclusions
Damage from improper installation, abuse, lightning or surge, freezing, or use outside published specifications.
