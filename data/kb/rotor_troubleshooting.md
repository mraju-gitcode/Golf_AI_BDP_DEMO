## Rotor pops up but does not rotate
Symptoms: the rotor rises and waters, but stays pointed in one direction.
1. Flush the rotor: remove the nozzle and run the station briefly to clear debris from the riser.
2. Check the inlet filter screen for sand or debris and clean it.
3. Confirm the arc setting has not been set to the minimum.
4. Confirm inlet pressure is within the operating range on the model's specification sheet.
5. If the drive still does not turn, the drive assembly may need replacement; note the model and date code before contacting support.

## Rotor does not pop up fully
1. Check for low pressure on the lateral line or too many stations running at once.
2. Check for debris around the case and wiper seal.
3. Confirm the valve-in-head is opening fully.

## Adjusting the arc on part-circle rotors
1. Turn the station on so the rotor is operating.
2. Rotate the turret to the fixed left stop.
3. Use the adjustment tool in the arc slot to widen or narrow the arc.
4. Observe one full rotation and readjust if needed.
