## Summary
Returned TEST-7710 rotors from lot TEST-2291 stop rotating after one season. Root cause: drive gear wear from an out-of-spec gear material batch.

## Affected courses in the field sample
Lakeview Golf Club and Pine Hollow Golf Club reported units from this lot.

## Corrective action
Quality hold on lot TEST-2291 inventory. Replacement internal assemblies from corrected lots. See WCN-2026-03.
