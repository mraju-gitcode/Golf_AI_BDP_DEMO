## Seasonal maintenance schedule
Before the season: flush laterals, check every rotor for full pop-up and rotation, and confirm arc and radius settings.
Monthly in season: inspect wiper seals and nozzles, and clear grass and thatch from around the case.
Before winterization: drain or blow out lines per the course winterization plan.

## Winterization and spring start-up
1. Close the main supply and open the drain points.
2. Blow out lines at low pressure with the rotors cycling one station at a time.
3. In spring, refill slowly to avoid water hammer, then run each station and check rotation.

## Replacing the drive assembly
1. Shut off water to the rotor and remove the snap ring.
2. Lift out the internal assembly and note the model and date code.
3. Install the replacement assembly, reset the arc, and run a full rotation test.
