## Warranty returns
1. Customer sends the warranty information form with photos.
2. CSR checks the claim against the warranty policy, change notices and validation questions.
3. CSR approves or rejects and issues an RGA number if parts must come back.
4. Replacement ships on a no-charge order.

## Non-warranty returns
Overstock and wrong-item returns need a return goods request (RGR) and are subject to a restocking fee. These are handled by Customer Service, not the warranty team.
